1## Purpose
QuizMaster lets people test their knowledge of US presidents. Each round gives 10 questions in a random order and ends with a score and an explanation for every answer.

2## Value to users
Prompts to answer in your own words and test your knowledge and then teaches
- Who is it for? (students, quiz fans, anyone curious about US history)
- What do they learn? (the explanations teach them something even when they get a question wrong)
- Why is it easy to use? (clear instructions, one question at a time, works on any device)
